# Wedillybird Brand Voice

Wedillybird speaks like a calm wedding operations partner: precise, human and elegant.

Core voice:

- practical before poetic;
- warm without being sugary;
- direct about friction: spreadsheets, scattered RSVP, manual relaunches, day-of check-in;
- respectful of couples, families and wedding professionals;
- no fake urgency, no exaggerated promises.

Preferred language:

- "invitation WhatsApp"
- "RSVP en temps reel"
- "check-in QR"
- "galerie partagee"
- "un seul lien personnalise"
- "pour les couples et les wedding planners"

Avoid:

- invented usage numbers;
- invented testimonials;
- generic AI/SaaS jargon;
- claiming legal, deliverability or conversion certainty;
- using guest personal data in marketing.
