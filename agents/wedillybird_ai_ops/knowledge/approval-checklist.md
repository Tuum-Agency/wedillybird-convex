# Approval Checklist

Before anything leaves the AI workspace, a human must check:

- Is this draft aligned with the current offer?
- Are all public numbers sourced?
- Are testimonials and logos real and approved?
- Is there any personal data from guests or clients?
- Does the message make a legal, payment, deliverability or compliance promise?
- Is the CTA correct for the current product state?
- Has the correct human owner approved publication or sending?
