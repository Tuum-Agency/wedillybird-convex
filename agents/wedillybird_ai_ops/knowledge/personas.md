# Personas

## Couple organizing their wedding

Needs:

- send invitations without creating a new app for guests;
- track RSVP and accompanying guests;
- avoid manual relaunches;
- keep day-of entrance fluid;
- collect and keep photos after the event.

Objections:

- "Will older guests understand?"
- "Can I customize the page?"
- "What happens if internet is bad on the day?"
- "Is WhatsApp required?"

## Wedding planner / agency

Needs:

- manage multiple weddings;
- keep agency branding visible;
- reduce manual RSVP operations;
- give clients a professional experience;
- avoid scattered tools.

Objections:

- "Can I use my brand?"
- "Can my team collaborate?"
- "What happens when I exceed quotas?"
- "Can this handle large weddings?"

## Venue / partner

Needs:

- smoother guest arrival;
- fewer paper lists;
- more confidence in attendance numbers;
- a reason to recommend a modern planning tool.
