# Content Pillars

## WhatsApp-first invitations

The invitation should live where guests already talk.

## RSVP clarity

Couples need one place to see who is coming, who is accompanied and who needs a relaunch.

## Day-of operations

Check-in should stay simple for the team welcoming guests.

## Memories after the wedding

Photos should not disappear into many separate conversations.

## Professional stack for planners

Wedding planners need branding, repeatable operations and less manual coordination.

## Trust and compliance

No invented proof, no guest data used in marketing and no automatic public actions.
